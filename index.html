From application to departure

  1. 1

    Apply

    Application form, initial documents, DMW pre-deployment requirements, review of qualifications and an initial interview.

  2. 2

    Get matched

    We send your CV to employers and set up your employer interview.

  3. 3

    Get hired

    You receive and accept the job offer.

  4. 4

    Get ready

    Visa processing, employment contract, training, pre-departure orientation seminar, medical exam and OEC from the DMW.

  5. 5

    Fly

    Flight booking and briefing, airport send-off, departure kit, and monitoring after you arrive.

About ROMA

ROMA Overseas Manpower Inc. connects Filipino talent with employers abroad, from our office in Makati City.

Read our story

Mission

We aim to promote the welfare and success of Filipino migrant workers through personalized support built on trust and integrity. We strive to be the partner of choice of our stakeholders by focusing on quality and streamlined service while ensuring compliance to global recruitment policies and standards.

Vision

To be the pillar of Filipinos for overseas recruitment through innovative digitalization and integration of global trends. We provide meaningful opportunities that will uphold the highest value and dignity of our migrant workers.

Our core values

R Responsive Recognizing the importance of each connection, we value our stakeholders by providing top-notch service all the time. O Optimism In delivering exceptional service and positive results. Committed in bridging Filipino talents with the right opportunities. M Motivation Embracing the unique quality of Filipinos to thrive for excellence and prioritize the common good for the benefit of all stakeholders. A Accountability Upholding the highest standard of honesty, transparency, and integrity in discharging our duties and responsibilities.

Why partner with ROMA

Commitment to quality and efficiency. Tailored manpower solutions aligned with your workforce needs. Each job order gets a customized hiring checklist and a dedicated recruitment officer.

Continuous support and progress tracking. Dedicated recruitment officers follow every worker from application until the end of contract.

Competitive pricing. Rates designed to give employers the best return on investment without compromising on quality.
